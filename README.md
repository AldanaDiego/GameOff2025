# TODO Unnamed
Game made in Godot for Github Game Off Jam 2025

- `Game Off 2025`: https://itch.io/jam/game-off-2025

## About the game
- Drive through the desert looking for water, treasures and places to hide from the heat.
- Manage your water tank and temperature levels. Don't run out of water and don't overheat!
- Weather will continuously change. Heatwaves will cause your water to evaporate and your temperature to rise
